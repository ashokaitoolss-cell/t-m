# Who's making you say this?

A detective plays a terrified suspect his own recorded threats and makes him
repeat them. Each tape is a different voice: calm menace, fluent Hindi, real
tears. The suspect can do every one. When the detective finally asks who gave
him the words, the answer is "You did." The detective leans into the light.
Same face. His case file holds the whole performance, written line by line
with emotion tags.

## Cast (one actor)

- **ARJUN, the detective.** Dark navy shirt, sleeves rolled, steel watch.
  Face hidden until 0:35. Never raises his voice. Patient, almost bored: he
  has heard these takes before.
- **THE SUSPECT.** Arjun's voice given a body. Same face. Crumpled white
  shirt, top button open, sweat at the hairline, hair a little messier. He
  starts as the most frightened man in the room and ends as the calmest.

## Set and look

- **Room:** interrogation room, darkness beyond the walls. Steel table, two
  chairs, one low hanging lamp between them. The lampshade is what keeps the
  detective's face out of the light.
- **On the table from the first wide shot:** a blue Sony Walkman (no
  headphones), an evidence bag holding three cassettes labelled EXHIBIT A, B
  and C, and a closed manila folder stamped CASE NO. 11.
- **Palette:** cold, desaturated room with one warm pool of light. Only two
  things in the film are saturated: the blue Walkman and the purple tags on
  the case file page.
- **Camera:** each exhibit is shot tighter than the last (MCU, then CU, then
  ECU). The question and reveal open back up to a symmetrical frame with the
  lamp in the centre.

## Shooting script

### HOOK (0:00–0:06)

**1 · 0:00–0:01 · ECU, Walkman, top-down**
A finger presses PLAY. *CLUNK.* The reels start turning. Tape hiss.

> TAPE *(calm, menacing)*: I know—

**2 · 0:01–0:03 · CU, Suspect**
Cut while the line is still playing. The voice on the tape is ice cold; the
face under the lamp is shaking, eyes wet. He flinches at his own voice.

> TAPE: —where you sleep.

**3 · 0:03–0:04 · OTS from behind the detective**
The detective's shoulder and the back of his head, black against the dark;
the suspect beyond him, lit.

> DETECTIVE: You said this?

**4 · 0:04–0:06 · CU, Suspect**

> SUSPECT *(scared)*: Sir, please... *(gulps)* I didn't have a choice.

### EXHIBIT A (0:06–0:15)

**5 · 0:06–0:08 · Insert, detective's hands**
His hands rest flat on the closed case file, in the light. His face stays
above it, in shadow.

> DETECTIVE: Say it. Exactly like the tape.

**6 · 0:08–0:10 · MCU, Suspect**

> SUSPECT *(nervous, stammering)*: I... I know where you... sleep.

**7 · 0:10–0:12 · Insert, detective's hand**
One finger taps the table once.

> DETECTIVE: No. *(beat)* The way you said it.

**8 · 0:12–0:15 · Slow push-in, Suspect**
He lowers his eyes. The shaking stops. He looks up. Dead calm, identical to
the tape:

> SUSPECT *(calm, menacing)*: I know where you sleep.

*Sound: room tone drops out under the line.*

### EXHIBIT B (0:15–0:23)

**9 · 0:15–0:16.5 · Insert, Walkman**
Eject, *CLACK*. EXHIBIT B goes in. Door shut. PLAY, *CLUNK*.

**10 · 0:16.5–0:19 · CU, Suspect listening**

> TAPE *(whispers)*: आज रात दरवाज़ा बंद करना मत भूलना।
> *Subtitle: Don't forget to lock your door tonight.*

**11 · 0:19–0:20.5 · CU, Suspect**
Genuinely lost.

> SUSPECT *(confused)*: I don't even speak—
> DETECTIVE *(O.S., cutting him off)*: Say it.

**12 · 0:20.5–0:23 · ECU, Suspect**
He leans into the light and whispers it back in perfect Hindi, same voice,
same breath.

> SUSPECT *(whispers)*: आज रात दरवाज़ा बंद करना मत भूलना।

### EXHIBIT C (0:23–0:30.5)

**13 · 0:23–0:24 · Insert, Walkman**
One fast, fluid motion. The detective is speeding up too: *CLACK*, EXHIBIT C,
*CLUNK*.

**14 · 0:24–0:28 · CU, Suspect**
The tape starts sobbing. By the second "please", the suspect's lips are
already moving with it. His eyes fill. He joins in perfect unison, real tears
falling. Nobody asked him to this time.

> TAPE *(crying)*: Please...
> TAPE + SUSPECT *(crying, in unison)*: ...please, I'm begging you. *(sobs)* Just let me go home.

*Sound: the two voices merge into one. You can't tell them apart.*

**15 · 0:28–0:28.5 · Insert, Walkman**
The detective's finger presses STOP. *CLICK.* Dead silence.

**16 · 0:28.5–0:30.5 · CU, Suspect**
He wipes his face with the back of his hand. Drained.

> SUSPECT *(exhausted)*: I only said what I was told.

### THE QUESTION (0:30.5–0:35)

**17 · 0:30.5–0:33 · Wide, symmetrical**
The lamp in the centre. The suspect on the left, in the light. The detective
on the right, in shadow. The detective leans forward; his face stops just
short of the light.

> DETECTIVE: Who gave you the words?

**18 · 0:33–0:35 · CU, Suspect**
He doesn't look away. He's steady now. A pause.

> SUSPECT: ...You did.

*Sound: lamp hum only.*

### REVEAL (0:35–0:43.5)

**19 · 0:35–0:38 · MCU, Detective**
He leans into the light. Same face. He isn't surprised. The smallest smile.
He knew all along.

*Sound: one low sub hit as the light lands on his face.*

**20 · 0:38–0:39 · Symmetrical two-shot**
The same man on both sides of the lamp.

**21 · 0:39–0:40.5 · Insert, table**
He slides CASE NO. 11 into the light, opens it and turns it toward the
suspect.

**22 · 0:40.5–0:43.5 · Insert, case file page**
The speaker chip: "Arjun - My Voice". Tilt down through everything we've
heard (menacing, stammering, Hindi, crying) and stop on "You did." The last
line stays below frame.

*Sound: paper, then a faint tape hiss returns underneath.*

### BUTTON (0:43.5–0:47)

**23 · 0:43.5–0:45.5 · MCU, Detective**
Quiet, almost proud.

> DETECTIVE: You made me sound like a monster.

**24 · 0:45.5–0:47 · CU, Suspect**
For the first time, he's the calmest man in the room.

> SUSPECT *(deadpan)*: You asked for one.

HARD CUT TO BLACK on the last word.

### END CARD (0:47–0:51)

**25 · Black**
Half a beat of silence. The ElevenLabs logo lands on the Walkman's *CLUNK*.

> **ElevenLabs. Your voice. Whatever the role.**

*Optional:* the suspect's voice reads the line, warm and friendly. It's the
first time we hear that voice sound kind, which makes it one last demo.

## Performance notes

- **Suspect: his status climbs the whole film.** Lowest at 0:00, highest at
  0:47. Every switch has to be instant. Don't ease into an emotion: the snap
  from one voice to the next is the product demo, and the snap is the wow.
- **Detective: flat and patient.** He's never surprised, not even at "You
  did." His smile at the reveal is pride, not menace.
- **His lines are really direction notes.** "Exactly like the tape" and "The
  way you said it" should play as an interrogation the first time and as
  directing on a rewatch.

## Rewatch clues

- **A small scar across the left knuckle on both characters.** It's on the
  finger that presses PLAY at 0:00 and on the suspect's hand when he wipes
  his tears at 0:28.5.
- **The suspect knows the third tape's words before he hears them.** He's
  already mouthing along by the second "please".

## Sound

- No score. Room tone, lamp hum, tape hiss, and the Walkman's mechanics
  (PLAY clunk, eject clack, STOP click) are the rhythm section.
- A low drone creeps in at Exhibit B, rises through Exhibit C, and cuts dead
  on the STOP click at 0:28.
- Tape voice: slightly band-limited, with hiss and a little wow and flutter.
  The live repeat is clean and close. The performances are identical; only
  the source differs.
- One sub hit at the reveal (0:35). Nothing else is that loud.
- Master to −14 LUFS, true peak at or below −1 dBTP.

## Captions

- Burn in every line. Many people watch Reels muted, and the hook has to
  land on frame one without sound.
- Tape lines in italics, live lines in roman, so viewers can tell which voice
  is coming from the Walkman.
- Hindi: English subtitle ("Don't forget to lock your door tonight."), with
  the Devanagari small above it.

## Case file insert

Printed page clipped inside the folder, styled like the voice editor:
speaker chip, then each line with its emotion tags. Source and print-ready

`props/case-file-page.png` (preview).

```

[calm] [menacing] I know where you sleep.

[nervous] [stammering] I... I know where you... sleep.
[confused] I don't even speak—
[whispers] आज रात दरवाज़ा बंद करना मत भूलना।
[crying] Please... please, I'm begging you. [sobs] Just let me go home.
[exhausted] I only said what I was told.
[short pause] You did.
[deadpan] You asked for one.

—————— + Add speaker ——————
```

- "Arjun" is a placeholder: use the actor's or character's name. "My Voice"
  on the chip is what makes the connection click instantly.
- The same line appears twice with different tags (`[menacing]` vs
  `[nervous] [stammering]`). That's the payoff of "The way you said it."
- Frame the insert so it ends on "You did." The last line, "You asked for
  one.", stays below frame so it isn't read before it's spoken. It's on the
  page for anyone who pauses on a rewatch.

## Props

- **Walkman:** a blue and silver TPS-L2-style Sony Walkman, no headphones.
- **Cassettes:** three, in an evidence bag, stickered EXHIBIT A, B and C.
  Each swap gives a door *clack* that marks the beat change.
- **Case file:** a manila folder stamped CASE NO. 11 (a nod to the brand). It
  sits closed on the table from the first wide shot, so opening it later
  feels set up.

## Production notes

- **Same actor, both roles.** The detective's face is never seen before
  0:35, so a stand-in can play him in every over-the-shoulder and hands
  shot. Shoot all the suspect coverage first, then the detective's. The
  two-shot at 0:38 is a locked-off split screen, with the lamp as the seam.
- **If the film is AI-generated,** use one locked character reference for
  both roles and tell them apart only by wardrobe and hair.
- **The original Walkman has no speaker.** Viewers won't question it. For an
  airtight version, run a short cable to a small speaker just out of frame.
- **SONY / WALKMAN lettering is a third-party trademark.** If this runs as an
  official ElevenLabs ad, cover it or angle it away from camera.
