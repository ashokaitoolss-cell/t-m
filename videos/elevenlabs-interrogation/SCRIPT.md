# Who's making you say this?

A detective plays a terrified, chained suspect his own recorded threat and
makes him repeat it. When the detective loses his temper, the bulb starts to
flicker and the suspect cracks: every time the light comes on he's a
different man in a different language. Then a sobbing tape, and the suspect
cries along with it word for word without being asked. When the detective
finally asks who gave him the words, the answer is "You did." The detective
leans into the light. Same face. His case file holds the whole performance,
written line by line with emotion tags.

## Cast (one actor)

- **ARJUN, the detective.** Dark navy shirt, sleeves rolled, steel watch.
  Face hidden until 0:37.5. Patient, almost bored: he has heard these takes
  before. He raises his voice exactly twice, both in the flicker, and both
  times it's a direction, not a loss of temper.
- **THE SUSPECT.** Arjun's voice given a body. Same face. Crumpled white
  shirt, top button open, sweat at the hairline, hair a little messier.
  Handcuffed, with the chain run through a steel ring on the table. He
  starts as the most frightened man in the room and ends as the calmest.

## Set and look

- **Room:** interrogation room, darkness beyond the walls. Steel table with a
  welded steel ring, two chairs, one low hanging bulb with a shade between
  them. The shade is what keeps the detective's face out of the light.
- **On the table:** a blue Sony Walkman (no headphones), an evidence bag
  holding two cassettes labelled EXHIBIT A and EXHIBIT B, and a closed manila
  folder stamped CASE NO. 11.
- **Palette:** cold, desaturated room with one warm pool of light. Only two
  things in the film are saturated: the blue Walkman and the purple tags on
  the case file page.
- **Camera:** Exhibit A pushes in slowly. The flicker breaks every rule, with
  a new angle on every flash. After "ENOUGH!" the camera goes still and
  tight. The question and reveal open back up to a symmetrical frame with the
  bulb in the centre.

The film builds to the flicker, its loudest and fastest moment. "ENOUGH!"
kills it, and from there everything gets quieter and slower: a sobbing tape,
a STOP click, one question, and the silence before "You did." That silence
should be the loudest moment in the film.

## Shooting script

### HOOK (0:00–0:06)

**1 · 0:00–0:01 · ECU, Walkman, top-down**
A finger presses PLAY. *CLUNK.* The reels start turning. Tape hiss.

> TAPE *(calm, menacing)*: I know—

**2 · 0:01–0:03 · CU, Suspect**
Cut while the line is still playing. The voice on the tape is ice cold; the
face under the bulb is shaking, eyes wet. His cuffed hands grip the table
edge. He flinches at his own voice.

> TAPE: —where you sleep.

**3 · 0:03–0:04 · OTS from behind the detective**
The detective's shoulder and the back of his head, black against the dark;
the suspect beyond him, lit.

> DETECTIVE: You said this?

**4 · 0:04–0:06 · CU, Suspect**

> SUSPECT *(scared)*: Sir, please... *(gulps)* I didn't have a choice.

### EXHIBIT A (0:06–0:15)

**5 · 0:06–0:08 · Insert, detective's hands**
His hands rest flat on the closed case file, in the light. His face stays
above it, in shadow.

> DETECTIVE: Say it. Exactly like the tape.

**6 · 0:08–0:10 · MCU, Suspect**

> SUSPECT *(nervous, stammering)*: I... I know where you... sleep.

**7 · 0:10–0:12 · Insert, detective's hand**
One finger taps the table once.

> DETECTIVE: No. *(beat)* The way you said it.

**8 · 0:12–0:15 · Slow push-in, Suspect**
He lowers his eyes. The shaking stops. He looks up. Dead calm, identical to
the tape:

> SUSPECT *(calm, menacing)*: I know where you sleep.

*Sound: room tone drops out under the line.*

### THE FLICKER (0:15–0:25.5)

**9 · 0:15–0:17 · CU, detective's fist, rising into a medium OTS (one shot)**
The fist slams the table. *BANG.* The Walkman jumps, the chain rattles, the
bulb swings and flickers. Without cutting, the camera rises over the
detective's shoulder to a medium shot of the suspect.

> DETECTIVE *(shouting)*: You think this is a game?! WHO ARE YOU?!

**10 · 0:17–0:18 · OTS, quick push-in on the Suspect**
He flinches hard. His breath catches. Something switches behind his eyes;
his focus goes somewhere far away.

*Sound: a thin, high whine rises, like ringing ears. It keeps building
through the whole flicker.*

**11 · 0:18–0:23.5 · The strobe**
The bulb dies and stutters back to life, faster each time. Every flash is a
new angle and a different man. Black between flashes, and the dark cuts
every voice off mid-breath.

- **Flash 1 · 0:18 · Low angle, CU.** Tears, hands pressed together as far
  as the cuffs allow, begging.
  > SUSPECT *(terrified, begging)*: ¡Por favor, yo no fui!
  > *Subtitle: Please, it wasn't me!*
- **Flash 2 · 0:19.4 · Dutch angle, MCU.** On his feet, yanked short by the
  chain, furious, shouting into the dark.
  > SUSPECT *(shouting)*: तू जानता नहीं मैं कौन हूँ!
  > *Subtitle: You don't know who I am!*
- **Flash 3 · 0:21 · Top-down.** Both fists hammering the table, the chain
  snapping taut against the steel ring again and again.
  > SUSPECT *(screams)*
- **Flash 4 · 0:21.9 · ECU, eyes and mouth.** Perfectly still. He leans into
  the light and laughs, slow and menacing.
  > SUSPECT *(laughs menacingly)*: Я знаю, где ты спишь.
  > *Subtitle: I know where you sleep.*
- **0:23 · The stutter.** The bulb buzzes and stutters dim-to-bright. All
  four voices overlap: begging, shouting, screaming, laughing. The whine
  peaks.

**12 · 0:23.5–0:24 · OTS, the detective half-risen, a black silhouette looming into frame**

> DETECTIVE *(roaring)*: ENOUGH!

The bulb slams on and stays on. Every sound cuts dead.

**13 · 0:24–0:25.5 · CU, Suspect**
He's frozen mid-scream. His mouth snaps shut. Tears, shaking breath, the
chain still taut in his fists. Only the bulb's hum, settling.

### EXHIBIT B (0:25.5–0:33)

**14 · 0:25.5–0:26.5 · Insert, Walkman**
Calm again, as if nothing happened, the detective ejects the cassette and
slides in EXHIBIT B. *CLACK.* *CLUNK.*

**15 · 0:26.5–0:30.5 · CU, Suspect**
The tape starts sobbing. By the second "please", the suspect's lips are
already moving with it. His eyes fill. He joins in perfect unison, real tears
falling. Nobody asked him to this time.

> TAPE *(crying)*: Please...
> TAPE + SUSPECT *(crying, in unison)*: ...please, I'm begging you. *(sobs)* Just let me go home.

*Sound: the two voices merge into one. You can't tell them apart.*

**16 · 0:30.5–0:31 · Insert, Walkman**
The detective's finger presses STOP. *CLICK.* Dead silence.

**17 · 0:31–0:33 · CU, Suspect**
He wipes his face with the back of his hand. Drained.

> SUSPECT *(exhausted)*: I only said what I was told.

### THE QUESTION (0:33–0:37.5)

**18 · 0:33–0:35.5 · Wide, symmetrical**
The bulb in the centre. The suspect on the left, in the light. The detective
on the right, in shadow. The detective leans forward; his face stops just
short of the light.

> DETECTIVE: Who gave you the words?

**19 · 0:35.5–0:37.5 · CU, Suspect**
He doesn't look away. He's steady now. A pause.

> SUSPECT: ...You did.

*Sound: bulb hum only.*

### REVEAL (0:37.5–0:46)

**20 · 0:37.5–0:40.5 · MCU, Detective**
He leans into the light. Same face. He isn't surprised. The smallest smile.
He knew all along.

*Sound: one low sub hit as the light lands on his face.*

**21 · 0:40.5–0:41.5 · Symmetrical two-shot**
The same man on both sides of the bulb.

**22 · 0:41.5–0:43 · Insert, table**
He slides CASE NO. 11 into the light, opens it and turns it toward the
suspect.

**23 · 0:43–0:46 · Insert, case file page**
The speaker chip: "Arjun - My Voice". Tilt down through everything we've
heard (menacing, stammering, Spanish, Hindi, the scream, Russian, crying)
and stop on "You did." The last line stays below frame.

*Sound: paper, then a faint tape hiss returns underneath.*

### BUTTON (0:46–0:49.5)

**24 · 0:46–0:48 · MCU, Detective**
Quiet, almost proud.

> DETECTIVE: You made me sound like a monster.

**25 · 0:48–0:49.5 · CU, Suspect**
For the first time, he's the calmest man in the room.

> SUSPECT *(deadpan)*: You asked for one.

HARD CUT TO BLACK on the last word.

### END CARD (0:49.5–0:53.5)

**26 · Black**
Half a beat of silence. The ElevenLabs logo lands on the Walkman's *CLUNK*.

> **ElevenLabs. Your voice. Whatever the role.**

## Performance notes

- **Suspect: his status climbs the whole film,** with one dip: "ENOUGH!"
  knocks him to the floor right before the final climb. Every switch has to
  be instant. Don't ease into an emotion: the snap from one voice to the next
  is the product demo, and the snap is the wow.
- **The flicker: each flash is a complete, committed man, not a mood swing.**
  Fear, rage, a scream, a calm laugh. Hit each one at full intensity from the
  first frame and let the dark do the editing.
- **Detective: flat and patient, except for two shouts.** He's never
  surprised, not even at "You did." His smile at the reveal is pride, not
  menace.
- **His lines are really direction notes.** "Exactly like the tape", "The way
  you said it", even "WHO ARE YOU?!" should play as an interrogation the
  first time and as directing on a rewatch.

## Rewatch clues

- **A small scar across the left knuckle on both characters.** It's on the
  finger that presses PLAY at 0:00 and on the suspect's hand when he wipes
  his tears at 0:31.
- **The light obeys the detective.** It starts flickering when he slams the
  table and snaps back on when he shouts "ENOUGH!"
- **The Russian flash is the opening threat again,** in another language.
- **The suspect knows the second tape's words before he hears them.** He's
  already mouthing along by the second "please".

## Sound

- No score. Room tone, the bulb's hum, tape hiss, the chain, and the
  Walkman's mechanics (PLAY clunk, eject clack, STOP click) are the rhythm
  section.
- The flicker: a hard low hit on the fist, an electrical crackle on every
  flash, and dead silence in every dark gap, with each voice cut off clean.
  The whine builds under all of it, peaks in the stutter, and dies on
  "ENOUGH!"
- Tape voice: slightly band-limited, with hiss and a little wow and flutter.
  The live repeat is clean and close. The performances are identical; only
  the source differs.
- One sub hit at the reveal (0:37.5). After "ENOUGH!", nothing else is that
  loud.
- Master to −14 LUFS, true peak at or below −1 dBTP.

## Captions

- Burn in every line. Many people watch Reels muted, and the hook has to
  land on frame one without sound.
- Tape lines in italics, live lines in roman, so viewers can tell which voice
  is coming from the Walkman.
- Spanish, Hindi and Russian: the original line small on top, the English
  subtitle below. Each caption appears and disappears with its flash.

  one.", stays below frame so it isn't read before it's spoken. It's on the
  page for anyone who pauses on a rewatch.

## Props

- **Walkman:** a blue and silver TPS-L2-style Sony Walkman, no headphones.
- **Cassettes:** two, in an evidence bag, stickered EXHIBIT A and EXHIBIT B.
  The swap gives a door *clack* that marks the beat change.
- **Cuffs and chain:** the chain runs through a steel ring welded to the
  table. It must be long enough for him to stand and hammer the table, and
  short enough to yank him back before he reaches the detective.
- **Bulb:** a practical bulb on a dimmer or DMX so the flicker happens in
  camera, timed to the sound.
- **Case file:** a manila folder stamped CASE NO. 11 (a nod to the brand). It
  sits closed on the table from Exhibit A, so opening it later feels set up.

## Production notes

- **Strobe safety.** Keep full on/off flashes to three per second or fewer,
  and make the final stutter dim-to-bright rather than black-to-white.
  Faster, harsher strobes can trigger photosensitive seizures, and platforms
  can flag them.
- **Same actor, both roles.** The detective's face is never seen before
  0:37.5, so a stand-in can play him in every over-the-shoulder, fist and
  silhouette shot. Shoot all the suspect coverage first, then the
  detective's. The two-shot at 0:40.5 is a locked-off split screen, with the
  bulb as the seam.
- **If the film is AI-generated,** use one locked character reference for
  both roles and tell them apart only by wardrobe and hair.
- **The original Walkman has no speaker.** Viewers won't question it. For an
  airtight version, run a short cable to a small speaker just out of frame.
- **SONY / WALKMAN lettering is a third-party trademark.** If this runs as an
  official ElevenLabs ad, cover it or angle it away from camera.
