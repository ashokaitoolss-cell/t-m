# Blue Crayon — two short cuts

Two simpler versions of the film, each under a minute. Both keep the same ending: the robot
brings Papa the boy's last drawing, Papa breaks and hugs it, and the last shot is over his
shoulder onto the robot's eyes, fixed on the empty bed. The 90-second cut in `../SCRIPT.pdf`
and `../STORYBOARD.md` is unchanged until one of these is picked.

| | A — "Look" | B — "The Blue Sun" |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Length | 0:56, 13 shots | 0:51, 13 shots |
| Order | Start to finish | Starts after the boy is gone; the past is three 2-second flashes |
| Words | "Papa, look!" said twice: once ignored, once as the voiceover | None |
| The wound | Papa never looked at his son's drawing | The robot can't hold the yellow crayon, and no one is left to tape it on |
| The drawing | The boy's, half-coloured sun | The boy's half-yellow sun, finished in the robot's wobbly blue |

## A — "Look" (0:56)

The boy asks his father to look once, and his father doesn't. At the end the robot makes him.

## B — "The Blue Sun" (0:51)

No words. The film opens after the boy is gone. The robot finishes his last drawing with the
only crayon it can hold, the one he taped to its finger.

| --- | --- | --- |
