# Blue Crayon — short cuts

Three versions of the film, each under a minute. **C, "The Knock", is the recommended one.** It
is free to change the plot, and it opens on a hook instead of building up to one. A and B
are kept below for comparison. The 90-second cut in `../SCRIPT.pdf` and `../STORYBOARD.md`
is unchanged until one of these is picked.

| | C — "The Knock" | A — "Look" | B — "The Blue Sun" |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Length | 0:57, 16 shots | 0:56, 13 shots | 0:51, 13 shots |
| Hook | A man in a funeral suit throws a little robot out into the rain | A robot hiding in the rain | A robot alone at a child's table |
| Order | Present, then a flashback, then back | Start to finish | After the boy is gone; the past is three 2-second flashes |
| Words | "Give it to Papa... when he's sad." | "Papa, look!" said twice: once ignored, once as the voiceover | None |
| The wound | The boy knew his father would be sad | Papa never looked at his son's drawing | The robot can't hold the yellow crayon, and no one is left to tape it on |
| The drawing | Papa holding the robot's hand; the boy drew himself as the sun | The three of them under a half-coloured sun | The sun finished in the robot's wobbly blue |

## C — "The Knock" (0:57)

The door is the whole film. The boy opened it when a scared robot knocked. Now Papa won't,
and the robot keeps knocking because it still has one job: the boy's last drawing, and his
last words, "Give it to Papa... when he's sad." The drawing shows Papa holding the robot's
hand, with the boy up in the sky as the sun. The thing Papa just threw away is the thing his
son left him.

| 5 | 0:12–0:15 | Behind him, Papa shakes his head and points away. The boy grabs the robot's hand and pulls it inside. |
| 6 | 0:15–0:19 | **Drawing corner, lamplight.** The crayon slips. The boy giggles and tapes it on. *Rrrip.* The robot's eyes curve into arcs. |

## A — "Look" (0:56)

The boy asks his father to look once, and his father doesn't. At the end the robot makes him.

| # | Time | Shot |
| --- | --- | --- |

| 2 | 0:05–0:09 | **Close.** The boy holds out his palm. The robot rests one fingertip in it. Its eyes curve into arcs. |
| 3 | 0:09–0:14 | **Front door, night.** Papa looks up from his phone, sees the robot and points at the door. The boy stands in front of it, arms spread, chin trembling. Papa turns away. |
| 4 | 0:14–0:18 | **Drawing corner, lamplight.** The crayon slips from the robot's fingers. The boy giggles and tapes it on. *Rrrip.* |

| 6 | 0:22–0:26 | **Kitchen.** The boy holds up a drawing: **"Papa, look!"** Papa doesn't look up from his phone. The boy's arm lowers. The robot watches. |
| 7 | 0:26–0:31 | **Bedroom, grey rain light.** The boy, feverish in bed, colours a sun. His eyes close. The yellow crayon rolls off the bed. |
| 8 | 0:31–0:34 | **Same frame.** The bed is empty and made. The robot hasn't moved; the drawing is in its lap. |
| 9 | 0:34–0:37 | **Bedroom, night.** Papa on the floor against the little bed, black suit, tie loose. |

| 13 | 0:51–0:56 | **Over his shoulder.** The robot's eyes, wide and still, on the empty bed. Three of the four notes. Cut to black. |

## B — "The Blue Sun" (0:51)

No words. The film opens after the boy is gone. The robot finishes his last drawing with the
only crayon it can hold, the one he taped to its finger.

| # | Time | Shot |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 0:00–0:05 | **Drawing corner, grey morning, dust.** The robot sits at the little table beside an empty child's chair, the blue crayon taped to its finger. A flat red ball under the table. |
| 2 | 0:05–0:07 | **Flash, warm lamplight.** The boy giggles and tapes the crayon on. *Rrrip.* Four piano notes. |
| 3 | 0:07–0:11 | **Present.** Papa walks past in a black suit without looking at the robot. He goes into the boy's room and the door shuts. |
| 4 | 0:11–0:13 | **Flash.** Papa points at the robot; the boy stands in front of it, arms spread, chin trembling. |
| 5 | 0:13–0:15 | **Flash.** Boy and robot asleep on a pile of drawings at dusk. |
| 6 | 0:15–0:19 | **Present.** Under the tape roll, the last drawing: three figures holding hands, the sun half coloured in yellow. The yellow crayon beside it. |
| 7 | 0:19–0:24 | **Close on the hand.** The robot reaches for the yellow crayon. It slips. It slips again. No one is there to tape it on. |
| 8 | 0:24–0:29 | It looks at the blue crayon on its finger, then slowly colours in the rest of the sun. Blue. |
| 9 | 0:29–0:32 | **Hallway, night.** Its eye-glow is the only light. It pushes open the boy's door. |
| 10 | 0:32–0:37 | **Bedroom.** Papa on the floor against the empty bed. The robot holds out the drawing. He doesn't look. It waits. The glow falls on the paper. He looks. |
| 11 | 0:37–0:41 | **Insert: the drawing.** PAPA, ME, the robot, holding hands. The sun: half careful yellow, half wobbly blue scribbled past the lines. |
| 12 | 0:41–0:46 | **Close on Papa.** His jaw holds, then breaks. He pulls the robot into his chest and sobs. |
| 13 | 0:46–0:51 | **Over his shoulder.** The robot's eyes, wide and still, on the empty bed. Three of the four notes. Cut to black. |
