## Act 1 — The Ball (0:00–0:20)

**01 · 0:00–0:03 · Wide.** Busy market street, golden morning. Papa at a stall, phone to his
ear, talking over the vendor. The Boy bounces his red ball at his feet; it bounces off his shoe
and away.

**02 · 0:03–0:05 · Low, tracking the ball.** The ball rolls into a shadowed gap between stacked
crates and stops against a small metal foot. The foot flinches back.

**03 · 0:05–0:08 · Boy crouches → reverse.** The Boy crouches and peers into the dark. The
Robot is curled in the corner, knees to its chest, trembling, eyes small *(Fear)*.

**04 · 0:08–0:11 · Two-shot.** Slowly, with one finger, the Robot nudges the ball back. It rolls
into the sunlight and into the Boy's hands. He grins. The Robot's head tilts *(Curious)*.

**05 · 0:11–0:14 · Extreme close-up on hands.** The Boy holds out his small open palm. The
Robot's three-fingered hand hesitates, then rests one fingertip in it. Cut to its eyes: they
curve up into arcs *(Joy)*.

**06 · 0:14–0:17 · Medium.** The Boy tugs Papa's sleeve and points. Papa, still on the phone,
glances over — a battered robot — and gives one hard head shake: *no*. He takes the Boy's
wrist and walks him off.

**07 · 0:17–0:20 · Boy's point of view, looking back.** The Boy is pulled away, looking back
over his shoulder. The Robot steps half out of the shadows into the sun and lifts one small hand.
The Boy's eyes.

## Act 2 — The Crayon (0:20–0:47)

**08 · 0:20–0:23 · Night, apartment front door.** The Boy, in pyjamas, cracks the door open.
The Robot is on the doormat, rain-wet: it followed them home. The Boy puts a finger to his lips
and pulls it inside.

**09 · 0:23–0:27 · Drawing corner, lamplight. The ritual is born.** The Robot tries to grip a
blue crayon; it keeps slipping out of its stubby fingers. The Boy giggles, rips a strip of tape —
*rrrip* — and tapes the crayon to the Robot's finger. The Robot stares at its finger, eyes wide.

**10 · 0:27–0:29 · Over the paper.** The Robot draws its first wobbly line. The Boy covers his
mouth, laughing.

**11 · 0:29–0:31 · Doorway.** The light snaps on. Papa stands there, sees the Robot, and his face
hardens. He points at the front door.

**12 · 0:31–0:34 · Low angle on the Boy.** The Boy jumps up and plants himself in front of the
Robot, arms spread wide, chin trembling, eyes fierce. Behind his small arm, the Robot peeks out.

**13 · 0:34–0:36 · Papa.** Papa holds his son's stare, exhales hard, turns and walks off. It can
stay. He doesn't like it.

**14 · 0:36–0:41 · Ritual montage (4 fast cuts).**
- *rrrip* — the crayon is taped on again.
- Heads together over paper, both drawing.
- A drawing gets taped up; the wall is filling.
- Both asleep on a pile of drawings at dusk.

The 4-note theme enters.

**15 · 0:41–0:44 · Papa in the doorway, home from work.** Coat still on. His son is in fits of
laughter, the Robot is drawing a wobbly sun, and the wall is bursting with colour. The corner of
Papa's mouth lifts despite himself. He catches it, and it's gone.

**16 · 0:44–0:47 · Kitchen.** The Robot holds up its own wobbly drawing to Papa, a crooked stick
figure of him. Papa walks past without looking. The Robot slowly lowers the paper *(Hurt)*.
**This sets up shot 27.**

## Act 3 — The Fever (0:47–1:00)

**17 · 0:47–0:50 · Boy's bedroom, grey day.** The Boy in bed, flushed, damp hair, feverish. Papa
sits on the edge of the bed pressing a cold cloth to his forehead, frightened.

**18 · 0:50–0:53 · Bedroom doorway.** The Robot stands there carefully holding a glass of water
in both hands. Papa takes it without looking and gently shuts the door on it.

**19 · 0:53–0:55 · Same door, later.** The Robot is back, holding the Boy's blanket. Papa,
haggard, shakes his head, and the door closes again. The Robot faces the shut door. Its eyes dim.

**20 · 0:55–1:00 · Drawing corner, slow push-in.** The Robot sits alone at the little table. The
last drawing lies there unfinished: crayons scattered, the tape roll on its side, the red ball
under the table. Rain on the window, silence. The Robot just stares. *(Keep the drawing angled
away and unreadable.)*

## Act 4 — Empty (1:00–1:11)

**21 · 1:00–1:02 · Night.** Red and blue light pulses across the dark living room and over the
Robot's face. A siren fades. The front door clicks shut.

**22 · 1:02–1:05 · Front door, day, rain.** Papa comes in alone, soaked, in a black suit. He
doesn't look at the Robot and walks past it down the hall.

**23 · 1:05–1:08 · Time-lapse.** Sunlight and shadow sweep across the room again and again. The
Robot doesn't move. Dust gathers. One drawing peels off the wall and drifts to the floor. The
red ball has gone flat.

**24 · 1:08–1:11 · Dark kitchen.** Papa, unshaven, in an undershirt, sits among empty bottles,
lit only by the open fridge. He stares at nothing.

## Act 5 — The Drawing (1:11–1:30)

**25 · 1:11–1:14 · Drawing corner, night.** The Robot's dim eyes flicker brighter *(Resolve)*.
Servos creak as it lifts its head. It picks up the last drawing; the blue crayon is still taped
to its finger.

**26 · 1:14–1:16 · Dark hallway.** The Robot walks slowly, its eye-glow the only light, and
pushes open the Boy's bedroom door — the door that was shut on it.

**27 · 1:16–1:19 · Bedroom, mirror of shot 16.** Papa sits on the floor against the small
empty bed, bottle in hand, in darkness. The Robot holds out the paper exactly as it did in the
kitchen. Papa doesn't look. Then the glow falls on the paper, and he does.

**28 · 1:19–1:22 · Insert: the drawing, lit by the Robot's eyes.** PAPA (backwards P), ME in
the middle, the Robot — holding hands under a big yellow sun, a red heart over the Robot, Papa
smiling.

**29 · 1:22–1:26 · Close on Papa.** His jaw clamps and he tries to hold it in, but his face
breaks. He pulls the Robot into his chest and sobs.

**30 · 1:26–1:30 · Over Papa's shaking shoulder, onto the Robot.** The Robot's chin rests on
Papa's shoulder. Its arms hang at its sides, the drawing dangling from the taped hand. Its
huge eyes stay wide and still, fixed on the empty bed. It cannot cry. The 4-note theme plays
once, unfinished. **Cut to black.**
