## Intent

A 60-second vertical explainer on how Duolingo turned Duo the owl, and the guilt
of a missed lesson, into its growth engine. Built to the *2.5D Paper-Halftone
Explainer* style guide: one painted picture per phrase, a colored subject in a
muted world, constant drift, print texture on every frame, small italic-serif
captions, and sound on every visual change. The user wants it "extremely
awesome", with particular care for motion, taste, text arrangement and sound
design.

## Assets

- assets/vo.mp3 — the ElevenLabs voiceover (60.5 s, male "friendly guy next door"); drives every cut.
- data/words.json — word-level timings of the voiceover (Parakeet TDT), used to place every cut and caption.
- Style guide PDF — `2.5D Paper-Halftone Explainer — Style Guide` (texture stack, motion, captions, SFX rules).
- Reference videos EarnEdits_1 / EarnEdits_2 — the target look, pacing, SFX and music treatment.
- SFX library — https://genxi-media-sfx.netlify.app/ (user-supplied; pick only apt sounds).

## Customizations

- Imagery: 2D painted illustration, not 3D renders — painterly semi-realistic digital painting in
  the style of a vintage 1960s magazine illustration, soft oil-paint brushwork. Generated with Higgsfield.
- Duo (chosen by the user): the fluffy olive/lime-green painted owl, Higgsfield job
  `2c4a58fb-ee67-458f-9ef2-0c809361452a`. Every scene with Duo passes this job as its image reference.
- Timeline split per spoken phrase: one sub-composition per scene on the HyperFrames timeline.

## Notes

- No CTA in the script; the video ends on the line "Build a memory people can joke about."
- Loudness target −14 LUFS (both references measure −14.3 LUFS integrated).
- Captions: Instrument Serif, italic white with soft glow over pictures, roman near-black on paper cards, 1–3 words at a time, kept in the y≈420–1400 band.
