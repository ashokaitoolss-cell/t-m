How to read the boards

Panels follow the usual conventions: scene.shot numbering, frames drawn at the delivery ratio (21:9), a thin arrow next to the subject for any movement, the camera note on the frame edge, and action, dialogue, sound and the transition written under each panel. Each scene opens with an overhead plan showing where every setup sits, who stands where, and the 180° line.

Subject moveThin white arrow drawn next to whoever moves.
EyelineDotted line from the eyes to what they look at.
Camera noteAmber, bottom left. There are no camera-move arrows: every shot is handheld and never travels.
Setup on a planWedge shows lens direction; number is the shot.
180° lineThe axis between two characters. Cameras stay on one side.
Panel numberScene.shot, e.g. 2.3 is scene 2, shot 3. Size is top right.
