## Notes before you run it

- **Face uploads.** The Jimeng/Dreamina Seedance interface blocks uploads with realistic human faces. Ashok's references are AI renders from his Soul, but they are photoreal faces and may still be refused there. On Higgsfield, Seedance runs with character Elements and real-looking faces are fine. If an upload is blocked, drop @Image2, keep @Image1, and let the text carry the final beat.
- **Grade references.** Your five grade frames are saved in `reference/grade/` for the team. I did not put them in the upload list: three contain recognisable actors, and the location plates already carry the grade. If your build takes a dedicated style image, use `reference/grade/02-dusk-shack-magenta-sky.webp` (no faces) or one of the location plates.
- **Dialogue density.** Four lines in 15 s is tight. If Muse's second line gets clipped in Prompt A, switch to Prompt B. Nothing else changes.
- **Rough cut timing.** The script slots this at 0:22–0:27. The spoken lines alone run longer than five seconds, so the edit will either overlap Muse's first line into the previous scene or the rough-cut timing stretches. Flagging it, not fixing it.
- **Voice.** The ElevenLabs Muse voice is still unpicked. Once it is, I render the four lines and they become @Audio1.
