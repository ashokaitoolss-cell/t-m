Ashok's Room — morning

Scenes 3, 4, 6, 8 · bedside arrival, the walk to the washroom, the desk, 11:02 PM
Plates: Soul Cinema · Grade: colour-negative, teal-green shadow / amber tungsten / golden sun · 1 Oct 2026
L1 · WIDE TOWARD WASHROOM
Master angle. Camera low at the foot of the bed looking down the length of the room. The walk path from bed to washroom door runs up the centre. Sun shaft from the left-wall window rakes the floor; the door at the far end glows cool with one warm bulb. Use for Scene 4 shot 1 and any "from behind" tracking.
L4 · REVERSE FROM WASHROOM DOOR
Reverse. Standing at the washroom door looking back at the bed. Sun flares into the lens from the window behind the bed. Muse's eyeline background in Scene 4; base for Scenes 3 and 8.
L2 · WASHROOM DOORWAY, ROOM SIDE
The threshold. Knee height, three steps back. The patch of floor where Muse waits. Slippers on the sill, towel on the back of the door, teal tile band and frosted window visible inside.

Continuity notes

  • Door opens inward into the washroom, hinge on the left as seen from the room
  • Muse's mark is the floor just left of the door, outside the sun shaft
  • Window position: L1 puts the window on the left wall; L4 puts it behind the bed. Treat as two windows, or lock one before scene plates. Recommendation: left wall (L1) is the key; L4's end-wall window stays for the flare shot only
  • Monitor shows a dim waveform, never readable text

The Washroom — morning

Scene 4 · brushing, the doorway, "Already did."
Plates: Soul Cinema · Character beat: Soul 2.0 · Grade: colour-negative, cyan window / amber globe sconce · 1 Oct 2026
L3 · INTERIOR, VANITY AND MIRROR
Master angle. Camera just inside the door at chest height, square to the vanity wall. One warm globe sconce above the mirror against flat cyan daylight from the frosted window top-left. Round brass mirror, opal globe sconce, floating walnut vanity with vessel basin and brass tap, glossy teal zellige band, reeded partition right.
A3 · DOORWAY BEAT, AS LIT
Lighting proof with the character. Ashok leaning out of the door: warm sun on the room side of his face, cool stone behind, the sconce catching his hair. This is the balance to hold for every washroom shot with a person in it.
L2 · THRESHOLD FROM THE ROOM
Entrance. Same door, same sconce, same frosted window, seen low from Muse's height. The terrazzo runs through the doorway without a break, which is what makes the two rooms one apartment.

Continuity notes

  • Door hinge is on the left from the room, so Ashok leans out to his right, toward Muse's mark
  • Muse never enters the washroom; her close-ups are shot from the room side at beret height
  • Mirror reflects the opposite stone wall and window only, no camera
  • Scale: the room is small. A 90 cm Muse on the threshold fills the lower third of a knee-high frame; keep her out of L3's frame entirely
