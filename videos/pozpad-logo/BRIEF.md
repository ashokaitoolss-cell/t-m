## Intent

A clean, minimalist logo sting for the PozPad app icon. Every move builds a piece of the
logo, then the frame holds on the finished icon, exactly as supplied:

1. A blue dot pops at centre and opens into the rounded-square tile (0.2–1.1 s).
2. The white pin grows out of its own tail tip (1.0 s); the blue P is written into it with one
   pen stroke, stem first, then the bowl (1.2 s).
3. The big dot runs one full orbit and draws the inner ring behind it, dropping the right-hand
   dot as it passes; the outer arcs sweep down from the top and drop the small dot (1.3–2.6 s).
4. "PozPad" rises letter by letter out of its baseline (2.4 s).
5. One soft sheen crosses the tile; a slow push-in to the end (3.3–5.5 s).

## Assets

- `assets/logo/pozpad-source.jpg` — the logo as supplied (609×616 JPEG).
- `assets/logo/pozpad.svg` — clean vector rebuild of it, generated by `scripts/build.py`:
  measured edges, fitted circles and Bézier curves, with the letterforms rebuilt from their
  measured stems, bars and corner radii.
- `assets/sfx/` — soft tactile cues from the GenXi library already used for the Clock It! film.

## Notes

- Light cool-grey backdrop so the blue tile reads; a blue-tinted drop shadow follows the tile's
  shape as it grows.
- The dots keep their exact positions from the source logo, so the final frame matches it.
- The source is 609 px wide; the rebuild centres it on a 616×616 square tile.
